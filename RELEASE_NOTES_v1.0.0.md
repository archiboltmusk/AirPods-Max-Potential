# AirPods Max Potential v1.0.0 — Gold-Standard Biometric & Spatial Suite

**AirPods Max Potential** turns your **AirPods Max**, **AirPods Pro 2**, and **Mac** into an uncompromising, on-device physical intelligence and bio-telemetry workstation.

---

### Highlights & Architecture

#### 🫀 Biomechanics & Spine Intelligence
- **C1–C7 Hansraj Vertebral Stress Model:** Calculates live cervical load in lbs/kg ($11.5\text{--}60\text{ lbs}$), disc compression percentage, and Newton-hours of cumulative postural burden.
- **Apple 360° Studio & Spine Telemetry:** Real-time 3D SceneKit human head and vertebral column rendering with interactive earwear switching (`AirPods Max`, `AirPods Pro 2`, and `Pure Sculpt`).
- **Autonomous Zero-Touch Calibration:** Automatically estimates world gravity baseline without requiring manual buttons, with optional manual upright calibration (⌘⇧C).

#### 🎧 Spatial Audio Biofeedback & Haptics
- **Soundstage Gravitation:** Acoustic occlusion filter ($20\text{kHz} \to 650\text{Hz}$) dynamically muffles and spatially tethers audio toward your calibrated screen center when slouching.
- **Directional Synthetic Ear-Taps:** Synthesizes resonant acoustic micro-clicks in the left or right ear to physically nudge lateral head tilt.

#### 👁️ Optical Continuity Sentinel (FaceTime HD / Continuity Camera)
- **Apple Neural Engine Vision Loop:** Seamlessly takes over posture tracking at 30 FPS when AirPods are charging in their case.
- **Scale-Invariant Facial Proportions:** 76-point Vision landmark tracking with distance-independent pitch, roll, and yaw calculation.
- **Spatial-Style Neutral Calibration:** 1-click calibration zeros the camera's downward viewing angle to your natural sitting height.
- **Live Camera Viewport & Vision Reticle:** Interactive preview with bounding box tracking, eye crosshairs, and instant privacy shutter.

#### ⚡️ 7 Biohacking & Autonomic Vitality Engines
1. **Autonomous "Physiological Sigh" Vagus Reset:** Detects stress spikes (shallow breathing $>18\text{ BPM}$ + masseter tension) and guides Huberman dual-inhale + long exhalation resets.
2. **Vestibular-Ocular Reflex (VOR) Brain Fog Eliminator:** 45-second optokinetic reflex trainer to sync head motion with retinal stabilization and eliminate screen eye strain.
3. **Cervical Joint Position Error (JPE) Diagnostic:** Clinical PT test assessing suboccipital mechanoreceptor drift in degrees ($\Delta^\circ$).
4. **Bilateral Mastication Guard:** Analyzes chewing cadence and alerts on unilateral TMJ load imbalances ($>70/30$).
5. **Acoustic Symptom Sentinel:** On-device passive tracking of dry cough bouts, throat clearing, and breath depth.
6. **Circadian Light & Melatonin Production Shield:** Sunset-synchronized evening posture tightening ($+25\%$) and $432\text{Hz}$ Solfeggio soundscape.
7. **Laryngeal Speech Ergonomics & Glottal Fry Sentinel:** Real-time speaking fundamental frequency ($F_0$) monitoring with hydration alerts after 25+ minutes of continuous meetings.

#### 🎓 Student & Ergonomic Productivity Suite
- **Hands-Free Study Gestures:** Flip Anki flashcards and advance PDF lecture slides hands-free using natural nod and tilt gestures (`Space`, `→`, `←`).
- **Super-Learning Binaural Wave Engine:** $40\text{Hz}$ Gamma (Working Memory), $10\text{Hz}$ Alpha (Flow State), and $6\text{Hz}$ Theta (Concept Mapping).

### Installation
1. Download **`AirPods-Max-Potential-v1.0.0-macOS.zip`** from the release assets.
2. Double-click to extract **`AirPodsPosture.app`**.
3. Move `AirPodsPosture.app` to your `/Applications` folder.
4. Launch the app and grant Motion / Camera permissions when prompted.
